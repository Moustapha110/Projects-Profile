### 6. AuxBattles – Social Music Battle App 🎧🔥

**Fun, Competitive Music Game With Real-Time Voting**

- Users join a “battle room” and get a **random or chosen category** (e.g., “Best Throwback 2010s”, “Late-Night Study Vibes”).  
- Each round:
  - One person is randomly selected to play a song.  
  - Everyone else **votes** on who had the best pick.  
  - Players get eliminated until there’s **one final winner**.  
- Eliminated players can **still vote**, which keeps engagement high even after people are out.

**Focus Areas**

- Game design that feels **light, social, and competitive**  
- Real-time logic for **rounds, scoring, and elimination**  
- Building systems that are enjoyable, not just “useful”
